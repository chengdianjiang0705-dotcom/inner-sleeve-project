Original work & editorial choices

Inner Sleeve Archive is a student music appreciation project by Diancheng Jiang. The artist selection, listening route and visual direction connect creative context with specific details to hear. The record icon, page structure, listening prompts and interactive games were developed for this website.

AI tools supported research organization and early drafting, English language polishing, HTML, CSS and JavaScript preparation, and troubleshooting.

Release information and short quotations link to their sources. Listening commentary reflects editorial interpretation: an invitation to notice a vocal change, a texture or a shift in mood. No full song lyrics or music recordings are hosted here.

Article headings and body text use Arial or Helvetica; quotations use Georgia italics. The Home title and original white wordmark use DM Sans. Its SIL Open Font License is included with the local font files.

Listening on NetEase

Listening buttons lead to NetEase Cloud Music. A small preview keeps the recording title and artist visible before the external handoff. The archive stays open in its own tab.

Verified catalogue entries use direct song links. When a recording cannot be matched reliably, the button leads to a clearly identified NetEase title-and-artist search. Factual release citations remain separate from listening destinations.
