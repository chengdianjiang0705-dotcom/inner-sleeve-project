The artist studies

Daniel Caesar

A soft voice, a difficult question.

Daniel Caesar is a Canadian singer and songwriter whose records bring R&B into conversation with soul and gospel. Freudian arrived in 2017, followed by CASE STUDY 01 in 2019, NEVER ENOUGH in 2023, and Son Of Spergy in 2025. Across these records, he returns to love, doubt, faith, and the ways a person understands himself through other people. I find his singing useful for close listening because a gentle surface can hold a difficult thought. A phrase may sound reassuring at first, while its timing or repetition makes the feeling underneath seem much less settled.
