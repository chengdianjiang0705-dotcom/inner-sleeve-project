The artist studies

Tyler, The Creator

Bright arrangements, private doubts.

Tyler, The Creator is a rapper, singer, producer, and director whose records connect music with a distinct visual world. Flower Boy, released in 2017, places loneliness, desire, and insecurity inside arrangements that draw on rap, soul, and jazz. IGOR followed in 2019 with a stronger emphasis on singing and synthesizers, while CHROMAKOPIA arrived in 2024. What interests me is the relationship between a song's surface and its speaker. A bright keyboard part or inviting hook may sit beside a confession that is harder to hear. Listening closely means paying attention to both at once.
